Poster & Mini-Campaign

This campaign uses the concept of a life story to show how one decision can change everything. The slogan “Don’t Let One Choice Rewrite Your Whole Story” emphasizes the long-term consequences of drug use. It illustrated both a bright future and a dark outcome to represent the health, social, and legal risks involved. The message encourages people to protect their future by making wise and responsible choices.

Not Just Rules, But Roots.

True guidance isn’t only about rules to follow — it’s the care, support, and wisdom that form the roots beneath every choice. These artworks show how family, school, and community nurture strength, helping students grow into their best selves and make decisions that shape bright, healthy futures.
